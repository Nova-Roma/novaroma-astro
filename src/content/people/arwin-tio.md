## About

Arwin Tio is a Senior Software Engineer at Cruise (General Motors), where he works on data processing systems for autonomous vehicles. He brings over a decade of experience building large-scale distributed systems, having previously served as Staff Software Engineer at NextRoll where he led the Data Products Team.

A Computer Science graduate from the University of Waterloo (2010–2015), Arwin combines deep technical expertise in data engineering, distributed computing, and systems architecture with practical experience delivering production systems at scale.
