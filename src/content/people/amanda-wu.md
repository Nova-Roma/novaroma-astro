## About

Amanda Wu is a product leader with 10 years of experience in highly regulated sectors. She participated in drafting a policy whitepaper advising government design and technology departments on responsible AI use, and advised the UK government's digital service team from a multidisciplinary lens.

Her work bridges product strategy, policy design, and responsible technology deployment — bringing a systems perspective to how organisations can adopt AI while maintaining accountability, transparency, and public trust.
