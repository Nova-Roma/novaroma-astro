## About

Larry Smith is a Professor of Economics at the University of Waterloo, known for his expertise in entrepreneurship, innovation ecosystems, and the economics of technology adoption. His teaching and research focus on how individuals and institutions can navigate technological disruption to create lasting economic value.

He advises Nova Roma on knowledge economics, institutional adoption strategy, and the workforce development implications of AI-native learning systems.
