## Demo Components

### 1. Time Horizon Visualization

3-horizon scenario navigation:

- **3–10 years:** Sovereign capacity as talent retention mechanism
- **10–30 years:** Climate migration infrastructure (AI compute today → population support tomorrow)
- **50 years:** Global leadership in clean AI (energy geography as competitive moat)

Interactive: select a horizon → see the state of energy / compute / talent dynamics for that period.

### 2. ForesightScope™

A cone of time representing alternative histories, future possibilities, and scenarios. Each scenario within the scope can serve as a basis for evaluating business tools and strategic positions.

- Collection of scenarios with their trajectories, timelines, and dependencies
- Scenarios relate to each other — branching possibilities from decision points
- Each scenario provides a "reality" context for running business tool evaluations
- **Amplified time axis:** AI-assisted weak signal detection provides an additional temporal dimension for all evaluations

### 3. Business Strategy Tools (Temporal)

- **Temporal Business Model Canvas:** BMC that evolves with time slider — shows revenue model shifts from pilot → scale → leadership
- **Strategy Maps over Time:** Strategic positioning changes per scenario selection
- **Porter's Five Forces over Time:** Competitive landscape evolution across the ForesightScope
- **Build-to-Manage Revenue Model:** Energy companies transition from power generation to energy+compute providers

### 4. Investment Vehicle Prototype (Blockchain)

A mechanism for Canadians to invest in sovereign distributed energy+AI infrastructure:

- Tokens collateralized against physical renewable energy + compute infrastructure
- Economic flywheel: investment → more infrastructure → increased backing value
- Utility: tokens redeemable for compute time on sovereign distributed network
- All assets physically in Canada, under Canadian law
