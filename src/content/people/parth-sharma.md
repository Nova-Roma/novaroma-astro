## About

Parth Sharma is a Computer Science student at the University of Waterloo with research interests in data privacy, AI ethics, and entrepreneurship. He was a finalist in the Quantum Valley Investments Problem Pitch Competition, presenting work on the terms and conditions problem — making data privacy decisions more transparent and accessible for users.

His current work focuses on the intersection of technology and user rights, exploring how AI systems can be designed to respect and communicate privacy implications rather than obscure them behind impenetrable legal language.
